What a transition does

Stage adds the incoming certificate beside the current one, and both are trusted for the overlap. It is refused if the certificate does not pass the same gate a pin does, and the fingerprint is re-derived from the certificate rather than taken from what you typed.

Activate promotes the staged certificate and drops the one it replaced. Abandon drops the staged certificate and keeps the current one - which is what you want when the incoming certificate turns out to be wrong.

Each act records its reason in the Colony's log, so an incident review can answer why a Comb's identity changed.
